# WIP Guidelines of MagnetarOrpheus

## Work-In-Progress (WIP) Policies
*   **Limit:** No more than **2** tasks in `in_progress` per person/agent.
*   **Focus:** Complete current tasks before moving to new ones in the `PLAN.md`.
*   **Context:** Every `in_progress` task must have a corresponding entry in `BITACORA.md` explaining the current status if left overnight.
